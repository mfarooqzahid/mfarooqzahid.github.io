## **Overview**  
**Otaku TV** is a **premium anime streaming platform** where users can **watch exclusive series shared by administrators**. It provides a **seamless viewing experience**, a **referral system**, and **personalized recommendations** to enhance user engagement.

## **Key Features**  
### **Watch Featured Series**  
- Access a curated collection of anime and exclusive content.  
- Stream high-quality videos with smooth playback.  

### **Manage Favorite Series**  
- Save and organize favorite series for easy access.  
- Get notified about new episodes and updates.  

### **Search and Explore Series**  
- Advanced search filters to find specific genres, trending series, and new releases.  
- Personalized recommendations based on watch history.  

### **Referral System**  
- Invite friends and earn rewards for successful referrals.  
- Grow the community while benefiting from the platform.  

### **Manage Viewing History**  
- Keep track of watched episodes and continue from where you left off.  
- Sync history across multiple devices.  

### **Purchase Series and Diamonds**  
- Unlock premium content by purchasing series or using in-app currency (diamonds).  
- Secure and flexible payment options.
