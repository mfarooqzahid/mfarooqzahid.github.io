## **Overview**  
**QuizVerse** is an interactive quiz platform that enables users to **learn, compete, and contribute quizzes** to the community. With **email & Google authentication**, a **leaderboard**, and **exam-taking features**, it provides an engaging way to expand knowledge.  

## **Key Features**  

### 🔐 **Authentication**  
- **Email & Password Login/Signup**  
- **Google Authentication for Quick Access**  

### 🏆 **Leaderboard & User Progress**  
- Tracks **user scores & ranking** in real-time  
- Displays **top performers** in different categories  

### 📚 **Explore & Take Quizzes**  
- Access quizzes **contributed by the community**  
- **Take exams** to test your knowledge  

### ✏️ **Contribute & Manage Quizzes**  
- Users can **create, edit, and publish** quizzes  
- **Admin moderation** ensures quality content  

### 📊 **Performance Insights**  
- Track your **quiz history & improvement over time**  
- Personalized **recommendations** for learning  

## **Tech Stack**  
- **Frontend:** Flutter (with Provider for state management)  
- **Backend:** Firebase (Firestore for database, Firebase Authentication)

## **Conclusion**  
QuizVerse **empowers users to learn, compete, and contribute** through quizzes—making education fun, interactive, and community-driven.
