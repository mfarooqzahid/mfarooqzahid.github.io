## **Overview**  
**MX Bill Pay** is a **secure and accessible bill payment solution** designed to provide a seamless transaction experience. The app allows users to **manage and pay multiple types of bills** while ensuring reliability and ease of use.  

## **Project Goals**  
The primary objective was to **enhance app accessibility and transaction reliability** by addressing existing issues and improving overall user experience.  

## **Solutions & Improvements**  
To achieve the project goals, several enhancements and fixes were implemented:  
- **Updated app theme** for better accessibility and UI consistency.  
- **Resolved API response issues** to ensure smooth and accurate data retrieval.  
- **Fixed payment processing bugs** to prevent transaction failures.  
- **Enhanced authentication system** for secure and seamless user logins.  
- **Improved app graphics and design** to provide a better user experience.  

## **Impact & Results**  
- **Improved accessibility** to essential bill payment features.  
- **Increased transaction reliability**, reducing failures and errors.  
- **Enhanced user experience**, leading to higher satisfaction and engagement.  
- **Streamlined and secure payment process**, ensuring smoother transactions.  

## **Tech Stack**  
- **Frontend:** Flutter (with getx for state management)  
- **Backend:** Firebase & REST APIs   
- **Payments:** Secure integration for bill processing  

## **Conclusion**  
MX Bill Pay **transforms digital bill payments** by ensuring a **secure, reliable, and user-friendly** experience. The improvements made significantly enhanced transaction success rates and usability.
